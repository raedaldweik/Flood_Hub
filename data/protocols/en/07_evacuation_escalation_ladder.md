# Evacuation Escalation Ladder

## 1. Purpose

Urban flash flooding rarely requires mass evacuation; it requires the right small movement at the right moment. This ladder defines five escalating steps so that operators, civil defense crews and the Rafid assistant use the same vocabulary when discussing evacuation.

## 2. Step 0 - Shelter in place (default)

2.1. For most residential buildings the safest action during a RED alert is to stay inside and move to upper floors. Moving people into flooded streets is the most common cause of avoidable harm.

2.2. Public messaging at this step: "Stay indoors or on upper floors."

## 3. Step 1 - Vertical evacuation

3.1. Ordered for buildings with occupied basements or ground-floor sleeping quarters, including labour accommodation in the Industrial Area, when zone water depth reaches 15 cm.

3.2. Occupants move to upper floors; building management confirms lifts are switched off and stairwells are lit.

## 4. Step 2 - Targeted relocation

4.1. Ordered for people who cannot remain safely in place: occupants of single-storey low-lying housing, stranded motorists in an underpass approach, and dialysis or oxygen-dependent patients whose supply is at risk.

4.2. Civil defense crews move small groups by high-clearance vehicle along an FOC-confirmed flood-free route to a pre-designated reception point on high ground.

4.3. Each movement is logged with the number of people, origin, destination and route.

## 5. Step 3 - Area evacuation

5.1. Considered only when a structural or utility failure, such as a substation fire or a collapsed culvert, makes an area unsafe regardless of water depth.

5.2. Requires approval by the duty commander and coordination with traffic police for a one-way outbound corridor.

## 6. Step 4 - Mass evacuation

6.1. Not a planned response to rainfall flooding in this city. Retained in the ladder for completeness and for compound events such as flooding combined with a hazardous materials release.

## 7. Decision support

7.1. The rules engine never orders an evacuation. Rules may raise an "evacuation review" action item at Step 1 or Step 2 conditions; the decision and the step chosen are made by the duty commander and recorded in the decision log.

7.2. The Rafid assistant may summarise the conditions relevant to each step and cite this document, but must not present an evacuation as ordered unless the decision log contains the order.

## 8. Reception points

Each zone file lists two reception points on high ground with capacity, access route and the facility contact. Reception points are opened by civil defense before Step 2 is ordered.
