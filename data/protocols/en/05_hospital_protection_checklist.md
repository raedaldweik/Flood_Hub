# Hospital and Critical Facility Protection Checklist

## 1. Purpose

Hospitals, dialysis centres and clinics must remain operational throughout a flood event. This checklist is worked through by the facility flood warden together with the FOC liaison when the facility's zone reaches forecast risk 60 or higher.

## 2. Twelve hours before

2.1. Confirm the emergency generator has a minimum of 72 hours of fuel and that the fuel tank vents are above the projected flood line.

2.2. Test the transfer switch and verify that the intensive care, operating theatres and blood bank are on protected circuits.

2.3. Move oxygen cylinders, pharmacy stock and records stored at or below ground level to upper floors.

2.4. Place sandbags or modular barriers at the emergency department entrance, the ambulance bay and the generator room per the barrier placement guide.

2.5. Confirm that at least one road approach to the emergency department does not pass through an underpass.

## 3. Six hours before

3.1. Recall essential staff whose commute crosses a zone at ORANGE or higher, so that they arrive before the rain.

3.2. Stage a pump unit and a tanker crew within 500 m of the facility. The FOC dispatcher records the staging in the decision log.

3.3. Brief the ambulance service on the current diversion routes.

## 4. During the storm

4.1. The facility warden reports water level at the lowest entrance to the FOC every thirty minutes.

4.2. If water enters a basement housing electrical or medical gas systems, the warden requests immediate pump priority; the FOC treats a hospital basement as a RED priority irrespective of zone score.

4.3. Elective procedures are postponed when the facility's zone is at RED.

## 5. Patient movement

5.1. Patients are moved vertically within the building, never out of the building, unless structural or electrical safety is compromised.

5.2. Any external evacuation follows the evacuation escalation ladder and requires FOC coordination for a flood-free route.

## 6. After the storm

6.1. Electrical systems that were wet are inspected before re-energising.

6.2. The warden and the FOC liaison sign off a short after-action note that feeds the next revision of this checklist.
