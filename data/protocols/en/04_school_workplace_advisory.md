# School and Workplace Advisory Procedure

## 1. Purpose

This procedure sets out how the Flood Operations Center recommends school and workplace advisories to the responsible authorities before and during heavy rainfall. The FOC does not close schools itself; it provides a timed, zone-level recommendation with the evidence behind it.

## 2. Decision timeline

2.1. **T-24 h.** If the forecast indicates heavy rain, the FOC issues a "monitor" notice to the education and labour liaison officers. No public communication yet.

2.2. **T-12 h.** If forecast zone risk is 60 or higher for any zone containing schools, the FOC recommends remote learning for schools in that zone and flexible hours for non-essential workplaces.

2.3. **T-6 h.** If forecast risk is 80 or higher, the FOC recommends closure of schools and remote work for the affected zones, and asks employers of essential workers to plan safe transport.

2.4. Recommendations are timed so that families receive them before 20:00 the previous evening whenever possible.

## 3. Content of a recommendation

3.1. Zones affected, listed by name in Arabic and English.

3.2. The forecast risk score and the rule identifier that produced the recommendation.

3.3. The recommended measure: monitor, remote learning, delayed start, or closure.

3.4. The earliest review time at which the recommendation will be reconsidered.

## 4. During the storm

4.1. If a RED alert is issued while schools are in session, the FOC recommends that students remain inside until the alert is downgraded. Early dismissal into flooding streets is more dangerous than staying in a safe building.

4.2. Employers in RED zones are advised to hold staff on site and not to release traffic onto flooded roads.

## 5. Public messaging

5.1. Advisories are drafted using the approved SMS templates and are published only after review by the communications officer.

5.2. Every message states the source of the data and the time of the next update.

## 6. Review and stand-down

6.1. The FOC reviews every active advisory at each rules-engine cycle and formally recommends a stand-down when all affected zones are below YELLOW for three consecutive hours.

6.2. Stand-down messages thank the public for their cooperation and give the reopening time.
