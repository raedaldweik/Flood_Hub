# Sandbag and Temporary Barrier Placement Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains where and how to place sandbags and modular temporary barriers to protect critical facilities before a forecast storm. Barriers are a preparedness measure: they must be in place before rainfall begins, because they cannot be built safely in moving water.

## 2. Priority list

2.1. Barriers are placed in the following order when resources are limited: hospital emergency entrances and generator rooms; electrical substations and transformer enclosures; metro and tunnel entrances; ground-floor data and telecommunications rooms; underpass pump chambers; schools scheduled to remain open.

2.2. The FOC issues the barrier task list when a zone reaches forecast risk 60 or higher within twelve hours.

## 3. Sandbag technique

3.1. Fill bags to two-thirds capacity so that they mould to each other. Do not tie the bags; fold the open end under.

3.2. Lay bags lengthwise and parallel to the flow of water, with the folded end facing away from the water.

3.3. Stagger joints like brickwork and tamp each layer flat. A wall higher than three layers needs a base at least three bags wide.

3.4. Place a plastic sheet on the water-facing side and tuck it under the bottom layer to reduce seepage.

## 4. Modular barriers

4.1. Water-filled or interlocking modular barriers are preferred for entrances wider than four metres and for anywhere that requires rapid removal afterwards.

4.2. Anchor the end sections against a wall or fixed post; an unanchored barrier will pivot when water pushes on it.

4.3. Check seals between modules after placement and again after the first hour of rainfall.

## 5. Drainage openings

5.1. Never block a functioning storm drain inlet. Barriers redirect water; they do not remove it. Leave at least one clear path for water to reach the nearest inlet.

5.2. Where a doorway sits below street level, place a small sump pump behind the barrier and route the discharge to the street drain.

## 6. Safety

6.1. Crews stop barrier work when water is above 15 cm or moving faster than a walking pace.

6.2. High-visibility clothing and gloves are mandatory; sandbags are heavy and abrasive.

## 7. Removal

Barriers are removed only after the FOC downgrades the zone below YELLOW. Used sandbags that have been in contact with floodwater are disposed of, not reused.
